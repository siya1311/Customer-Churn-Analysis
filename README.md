# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## Overview
Analyzed 7K+ customer records to identify behavioral and service-related factors associated with customer churn. The project uses Python for data cleaning and exploratory analysis, MySQL for customer segmentation and churn analysis, and Tableau for interactive visualization.

## Tools
- Python
- MySQL
- Tableau

## Business Questions
- What factors are associated with customer churn?
- Which customer segments have the highest churn rates?
- Which contract types have the highest churn?
- How does tenure relate to churn?
- What are the most common reasons customers leave?

## Key Findings
- Overall customer churn was approximately 26.6%.
- Month-to-month customers had a churn rate of approximately 42.8%.
- Fiber optic customers had a churn rate of approximately 41.9%.
- Customers with 0–6 months of tenure had the highest churn rate at approximately 53.3%.
- Electronic check customers had the highest churn rate among payment methods at approximately 45.3%.
- Customer service issues and competitive offers were among the most frequently reported churn reasons.

## Project Workflow
### Python
Used Python and Pandas to clean the dataset, check data quality, and perform exploratory analysis.

### MySQL
Used SQL to calculate churn rates and segment customers by contract, tenure, internet service, and payment method.

### Tableau
Created an interactive dashboard to visualize churn patterns and communicate key findings.

## Recommendations
- Focus retention efforts on newer customers during their first several months.
- Focus on high churn rate among month-to-month customers.
- Investigate customer satisfaction and competitive pricing/service issues among fiber optic customers.
